🤲 Daily Duas

Dua for Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

Quran 20:114

Dua for Goodness

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Our Lord, grant us good in this world and the Hereafter and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Quran 2:201

Dua for Repentance

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا

Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves.

From the supplication in Quran 7:23. Read the full verse for the complete dua.

Gratitude to Allah

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

All praise belongs to Allah.

A phrase of praise and gratitude.

Seeking Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ

I seek forgiveness from Allah.

A commonly used phrase of seeking forgiveness.

Trust in Allah

حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.

Quran 3:173
